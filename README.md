# HR Employee Survey Dashboard


## Dashboard Preview

![HR Employee Survey Dashboard](./HR%20Dashboard.png)


## Project Overview

The HR Employee Survey Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee satisfaction, engagement, and workplace experience across different departments within an organization.

The dashboard transforms raw employee survey responses into actionable insights that can help HR teams identify strengths, uncover improvement opportunities, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations regularly conduct employee surveys to understand employee satisfaction, engagement, and workplace challenges. However, large volumes of survey responses can be difficult to interpret manually.

This project was developed to answer key business questions such as:

- How satisfied are employees overall?
- Which survey questions receive the highest satisfaction ratings?
- Which areas require immediate improvement?
- Which departments demonstrate the highest employee satisfaction?
- How are employee responses distributed across satisfaction categories?

---

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Measure overall employee satisfaction.
- Identify top-performing survey areas.
- Highlight areas needing improvement.
- Analyze employee response patterns.
- Compare satisfaction levels across departments.
- Support HR management with actionable insights.

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains employee survey responses collected across multiple departments.

Key fields include:

- Response ID
- Question ID
- Survey Question
- Response
- Response Text
- Department
- Staff Count
- Manager Count
- Director Count
- Supervisor Count
- Employee Role

## Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and transformed before visualization using Power BI.

### Data Cleaning Steps

- Removed duplicate records.
- Checked for missing values.
- Standardized response categories.
- Verified data types for all columns.
- Renamed fields for clarity and consistency.
- Removed unnecessary columns.
- Ensured proper relationship between survey questions and responses.
- Validated department names and employee role classifications.

### Data Transformation

- Created measures for employee response counts.
- Calculated total survey responses.
- Aggregated department-level satisfaction scores.
- Generated rankings for survey questions.
- Created metrics for identifying improvement areas.

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

The dashboard includes four KPI cards displaying:

- Total Responses
- Number of Departments
- Number of Managers
- Number of Directors

### Top Survey Questions by Employee Satisfaction

Displays survey questions with the highest satisfaction scores, helping HR understand what employees value most.

### Top 10 Departments by Employee Satisfaction

Ranks departments based on employee satisfaction performance.

### Areas Needing Improvement

Highlights survey questions with lower satisfaction scores that may require management attention.

### Employee Response Distribution

A donut chart showing the proportion of responses across categories such as:

- Agree
- Strongly Agree
- Disagree
- Strongly Disagree
- Not Applicable

---

## Key Insights

### Employee Satisfaction

Most employees reported positive satisfaction levels, indicating a generally favorable work environment.

### Department Performance

Several departments consistently achieved higher satisfaction scores, suggesting effective management and employee engagement practices.

### Improvement Opportunities

Certain survey questions received lower ratings and represent opportunities for HR intervention and process improvement.

### Response Distribution

Positive responses accounted for the majority of survey feedback, while negative responses represented a smaller percentage of total responses.

---

## Recommendations

Based on the findings:

1. Maintain and replicate best practices from high-performing departments.
2. Investigate factors contributing to lower-rated survey questions.
3. Conduct follow-up employee engagement initiatives.
4. Strengthen communication channels between management and employees.
5. Monitor employee satisfaction trends regularly using HR analytics dashboards.
